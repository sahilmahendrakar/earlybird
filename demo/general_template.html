How this was recorded

Every system ran on the same audio in 80 ms steps; each change to its text and guesses was logged against the audio clock and is replayed here (compute time not added). Grey dotted words are guesses with their confidence, shown only above the slider's threshold. Each lane counts the words it showed correctly before the speaker finished saying them and never took back afterwards, either in its transcript or as a guess at or above the threshold. A word that flickers (shown, removed, shown again) only counts from the last time it appeared. A normal streaming recognizer gets many words this way too, by writing a word in its last few tens of milliseconds.

Clips: LibriSpeech test-clean (audiobooks), TED-LIUM (talks), People's Speech (public speech), and synthetic Kokoro speech; chosen by the builder as a spread where Earlybird anticipated several words. The numbers above cover full test sets.
