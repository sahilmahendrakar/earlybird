Earlybird, live

Press the microphone and talk. Earlybird writes each word before you finish saying it, guesses the next ones in grey, and shows how sure it is of every word.

The 16.8 MB 4-bit Earlybird model runs on a small CPU server; your audio is streamed to it in 80 ms steps and discarded. Confidences are the model's own probabilities, with no calibration step. Sessions are limited to two minutes.
