A replay of Earlybird's recorded output, not a model running in your browser: each recording was streamed through the 16.8 MB 4-bit model in 80 ms steps, and everything it showed was logged against the audio clock. Confidences are the model's own probabilities, with no calibration step; on our test sets, when it is 90% sure of a word it has not heard the end of, it is right 91–96% of the time.
